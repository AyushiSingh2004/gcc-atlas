Decision input
Candidate cities
Evidence scope: Savills (GCC leasing, 2020–2024) and Colliers (GCC leasing since 2021; report published July 2026). Neither source alone establishes total GCC count, comparable cost, or the best location for an individual company.

Decision implications

Established clusters

Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune account for nearly 70% of GCC leasing in Savills' 2020–2024 analysis.

Recent leasing evidence

Colliers reports Bengaluru and Hyderabad together account for over 60% of GCC space uptake since 2021 in its covered markets.

What this does not prove

Leasing is not a direct proxy for talent availability, salary costs, office rent or suitability for every function.

Evidence principles

What separates a source aggregation tool from decision intelligence.
01 / PROVENANCE

Every claim has a trail

Keep publisher, URL, publication date and the claim supported by that source together.

02 / COMPARABILITY

Align scope before comparing

Check definition, period, geography and unit before combining any two data points.

03 / UNCERTAINTY

Expose the unknowns

Use visible gaps and verification states instead of invented values or false precision.

!

Prototype limitation

This is a manually curated demo dataset. It does not continuously monitor sources, validate all company records or provide legal, tax or investment advice. A production roadmap would add source ingestion, entity resolution, freshness checks and human review.
